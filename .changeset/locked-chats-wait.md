Add the 16px `terminal-locked` and `chat-queued` icons for representing locked
terminal access and queued chat messages. React and styled Octicons provide
`TerminalLockedIcon` and `ChatQueuedIcon`; `@primer/octicons-react-symbols`
provides `TerminalLockedSymbol` and `ChatQueuedSymbol`.
